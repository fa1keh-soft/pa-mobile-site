Privacy and support

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 10 November 2026 · Android package: org.pamobile

This policy covers PA Mobile, an independent Android polar-alignment app for the standalone iOptron iPolar USB camera, and this support website. The privacy contact is fa1keh.soft@gmail.com.

PA Mobile processes camera images on your phone to detect stars and estimate polar alignment. It has no advertising, developer-operated analytics, cloud image processing, or PA Mobile account. Camera images and alignment recordings are not uploaded automatically to the developer.

Camera and USB access

Android camera permission and permission to access the connected USB device are required to receive camera images. Images are used for preview, calibration, and alignment. PA Mobile does not request microphone or contact access. You can revoke camera permission in Android settings and disconnect the USB camera to stop camera access.

Local storage and diagnostics

Camera controls and app preferences are stored on your phone. Manually saved captures contain images, dark frames, exposure and gain settings, timestamps, and diagnostic results. Optional Debug recording stores analyzed frames, exact darks, camera identifiers where available, phone model, app and Android versions, settings, and results. Debug recording is off by default. These sessions remain on the phone until deleted. Recording stops if available storage falls below its safety threshold or writing cannot keep up.

Exports and local deletion

The app does not send recordings to the developer automatically. Export session lets you choose a destination through Android’s file picker. If you choose a cloud-storage provider or later share the file, that provider or recipient handles the information under its own privacy practices. Review exports before sharing.

Delete recorded sessions removes debug recordings; manually saved captures are kept separately. Android’s Clear storage or uninstall removes the app’s private data, including preferences and locally cached purchase evidence. Files exported elsewhere must be deleted at their destination. PA Mobile cannot delete copies held by another service or recipient.

Location

The Google Play release does not request or use phone location and does not track location in the background.

Purchases and Google Play

The app is free to download. An optional one-time purchase unlocks alignment. Google Play handles payment and purchase restoration under Google’s terms and Privacy Policy. PA Mobile does not receive your payment-card details, bank credentials, or Google password.

The app exchanges product queries, purchase status, and purchase acknowledgments with Google Play. Google may process purchase information, device and app technical information, and billing-operation diagnostics to operate and protect these services. The Google Play Billing library and its supporting services are separate from the camera-image processing performed on your phone.

PA Mobile stores the signed purchase receipt and acknowledgment on your phone to verify access and allow offline use after activation. The receipt includes product and package identifiers, a purchase token, purchase status, and any order information supplied by Google. There is no developer-operated purchase server. Google retains its own transaction and service records independently of the app.

Deleting app storage removes the local receipt. Use Restore purchase with the purchasing Google Play account and an internet connection to recover access. Deleting the app does not cancel or refund a purchase. Manage purchases and request refunds through Google Play; contact support if activation fails.

Support emails and attachments

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any diagnostic files or other attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Support email is handled through Gmail, a Google service. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unrelated personal information.

Support emails and diagnostic attachments are deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved, or sooner if you request deletion. If applicable law requires a specific record to be retained, it is kept only for that obligation. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent, email fa1keh.soft@gmail.com. This does not delete Google’s independently maintained transaction records or copies you exported elsewhere.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and handles hosting data under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages contain no added analytics, tracking scripts, sign-in forms, or advertising. Following an external link or sending an email opens a separate service with its own privacy practices.

Security and changes

PA Mobile uses Android’s private app storage for settings and purchase evidence and Android permission controls for camera access. The developer does not sell personal information or use support information for advertising. Google Play, email, and hosting services maintain their own safeguards and retention practices.

This policy will be updated if data handling changes, with the date revised above. For privacy questions, contact fa1keh.soft@gmail.com.
