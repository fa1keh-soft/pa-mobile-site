PA Mobile

Polar alignment on Android

Privacy and support

PA Mobile helps you polar-align an equatorial mount using a standalone iOptron iPolar USB camera connected to a compatible Android phone.

The app processes camera images on your phone. It offers a free camera preview and a one-time purchase to unlock real polar alignment. It does not use the phone’s built-in camera for alignment or control mount motors.
